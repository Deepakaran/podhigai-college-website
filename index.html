Engineering Excellence Rooted in Values & Innovation

Established under the Podhigai Educational Trust, Podhigai College of Engineering & Technology has stood as a beacon of quality technical education in Tirupattur, Tamil Nadu.

Approved by AICTE, New Delhi and affiliated to Anna University, Chennai, our lush, modern campus combines state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure, world-class faculty, and industry-aligned training programs.

ADMISSION FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding admissions, eligibility, counselling, and campus life.

Our official Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions (TNEA) Counselling Code is 1525. Choose code 1525 during choice filling to select Podhigai College.

We offer 6 undergraduate engineering programs: B.Tech Information Technology (IT), B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI&DS), B.E. Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), B.E. Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE), B.E. Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE), and B.E. Mechanical Engineering.

Yes! Eligible students receive Tamil Nadu Government First Graduate scholarships, Post-Matric SC/SCA/ST fee waivers, BC/MBC welfare scholarships, and the 7.5% Government School preferential reservation quota benefits.
