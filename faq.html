Frequently Asked Questions

What undergraduate engineering programs are offered at Podhigai?

Podhigai College offers six 4-year B.E. / B.Tech programs: B.Tech Information Technology, B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, B.E. Computer Science & Engineering, B.E. Electrical & Electronics Engineering, B.E. Electronics & Communication Engineering, and B.E. Mechanical Engineering.

What is the TNEA Counselling Code for Podhigai College?

Our official Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions (TNEA) Counselling Code is 1525. Candidates attending single-window counselling should select Code 1525.

How does the Placement Cell prepare students for campus recruitment?

The Placement Cell coordinates multi-semester training modules covering quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, core technical problem solving, coding bootcamps, and mock interviews conducted by industry experts.

Are residential hostel facilities available on campus?

Yes, Podhigai provides separate, secure hostel accommodation for boys and girls on campus. The hostels feature 24/7 security, high-speed Wi-Fi, clean dining mess with hygienic meals, and indoor recreational areas.

What scholarships and financial assistance schemes are supported?

Eligible students can avail Tamil Nadu Government Post-Matric Scholarships (BC/MBC/SC/ST), First Graduate Tuition Fee Concessions, PMSSS schemes, and merit-based institutional fee awards.

How can I schedule a campus visit or admissions counselling?

You can visit our admissions office on Salem Main Road, Adiyur, Tirupattur directly from Monday through Saturday (9:00 AM – 5:00 PM), or contact our admissions helpline at +91 94880 01525 / +91 4179 292228.

Still have questions? Our admissions counselors are available to assist you.
